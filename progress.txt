### FEATURE-002: Implement Earth's axial tilt for accurate seasonal visualization
- **Status:** COMPLETED
- **Date:** 2026-01-24
- **Changes:** Added Earth's 23.4 degree axial tilt for realistic seasonal day/night patterns:
  - Added `AXIAL_TILT: 23.4` constant to config.ts
  - Added `AXIAL_TILT` property to EarthConfig interface in types.ts
  - Modified `initEarth()` in earth.ts to apply the tilt to both Earth mesh and clouds layer via `rotation.z`
  - Updated `updateEarthRotation()` to preserve the tilt when adjusting longitude-based rotation
  - The tilt affects how the day/night terminator appears at different times of year
  - Arctic region now correctly shows near-24h daylight on June 21 (summer solstice)
  - Arctic region now correctly shows near-24h darkness on December 21 (winter solstice)
  - Equinoxes (March 20, September 22) show terminator passing through both poles
  - Build completes without TypeScript errors
